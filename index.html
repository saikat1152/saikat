Short bio

I am an MSc student in Computer Science and Engineering at Dhaka International University and a Senior Officer (IT) at Janata Bank PLC, with seven years of professional software engineering experience. I completed my BSc in Computer Science and Engineering at Khulna University of Engineering & Technology (KUET) in 2016.

Building large-scale software sparked my interest in intelligent systems with smart, threat-preventive features. I am interested in building secured autonomous systems with artificial intelligence, machine learning and computer vision, along with their applications and explainability. Recent work and projects moved my career from industry applications toward research applications.

Research

Research interests: Artificial Intelligence; Machine Learning; Computer Vision; Autonomous Systems; forensics and security; road safety.

Projects

An iRAP-Inspired Relative Road-Condition Index from Street-Level Imagery and Visual Question Answering (MSc thesis)

A road-safety study that builds an iRAP-inspired relative road-condition index from Mapillary street-level imagery, OpenStreetMap road networks and an iRAP-style visual question answering protocol. Keywords: map matching, composite indicator, AI models, structured questionnaires.

Reinforcement Learning for Bowling Strategies in One Day Internationals

A Deep Q-Network approach to optimizing bowling strategies in One Day International cricket, published at ICCIT 2025.

BEFTN and RTGS at Janata Bank PLC

Bangladesh Electronic Funds Transfer Network and Real-Time Gross Settlement software built with Spring Boot and Oracle 19c. Its API communicates with the core banking software and Bangladesh Bank, and all Janata Bank branches use it.

Online Purchase and Sales Management System (BSc thesis)

An e-commerce site with an admin panel, central inventory management, payment gateway and cash-on-delivery ordering, SMS integration and CanvasJS ratings. Built with Python, Django and MySQL, and deployed on a VPS with Nginx, WSGI and Let's Encrypt.
